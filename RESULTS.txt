Blihat - Results summary
=======================
Date: 2026-10-10   Author: Dagimawi Moges Wakumo
Tracker: AdaptiveTracker, config configs/blihat_final.yaml (frozen 2026-10-09)
Benchmark: UAV123 (123 sequences) via got10k ExperimentUAV123; GPU: NVIDIA A100 (Colab)

1. Method in one paragraph
--------------------------
Training-free single-object tracker. A frozen DINOv3 ViT-L/16 (HuggingFace, final LayerNorm
applied to the hidden states) does the TRACKING: correlation of an anchor/long-term/short-term
feature memory over a multi-scale search region, relative quality gating, widening search and
tiled re-detection when quality drops. SuperPoint + LightGlue gives the BOX: the current crop is
matched against the frame-1 anchor and up to two keyframes, all correspondences are mapped to
frame-1 coordinates and one similarity transform (RANSAC) maps the exact frame-1 box into the
current frame. The LightGlue box is used whenever the fit is reliable (>=10 inliers, inlier
ratio >=0.3, size change <=1.2x per frame capped at 1.44x by the scale lock, center within 0.75
target sizes of the DINO center); otherwise the DINO box is the fallback. DINO memory learns the
observation at the LightGlue box; keyframes are added only from reliable fits on DINO-healthy frames.

5. Findings / limitations
-------------------------
  - The gain comes from box size: DINO (16 px patches, coarse scale steps) almost never
    rescales the box; the anchor-referenced LightGlue similarity fit does, without drift.
  - Non-rigid targets (people, wakeboarders) often fail the inlier-ratio check and fall back
    to DINO; a single similarity transform cannot fit a deforming body.
  - Closed-loop failure found and fixed: after a fallback stretch, one weak fit could shrink
    the box, the smaller crop cut off the target and later fits shrank further (person8_1:
    0.637 coarse -> 0.393). The scale lock (size check never switches off, step <=1.44x)
    fixes it (-> 0.701).
  - Aspect ratio is fixed to frame 1 in both paths (similarity transform); ARC is not addressed.
  - Weakest attributes: full occlusion, background clutter, low resolution - re-detection
    and tiny targets, not box accuracy.
  - Settings were selected on UAV123 subsets; DTB70 / UAV20L are held-out evaluations.

  Failure mode seen in the diagnostics - background lock (Girl2: coarse 0.036, final 0.025;
  the sequence fails with or without LightGlue, but the fine box shows the mechanism): the target is thin (34x92 px) and
  the frame-1 box contains a lot of background. As the camera pans, SuperPoint/LightGlue
  matches the BACKGROUND inside the anchor box with many inliers (60-120, ratio 0.4-0.5),
  so the fit passes every reliability check and the box slides with the background
  (IoU 0.91 at frame 4, 0 from frame 28 on) while DINO is overridden. The reliability check
  validates the geometry of the fit, not that it is on the target.
  Likely fix (future work, not applied - config is frozen): restrict anchor/keyframe keypoints
  to the foreground (the U2-Net mask already computed for the DINO kernel), and/or reject fine
  boxes whose center keeps drifting away from the DINO peak over several frames.
